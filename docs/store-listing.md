**App name:** FMBP – Find My Business Partner
**Subtitle / short description (80 chars):** Post what your business needs or offers. Find partners near you.
**Category:** Business · **Content rating:** Everyone · **Languages:** English, Hindi

## Long description (English)
FMBP helps anyone who runs or wants to run a business in India find the people they need, close by.

Need money to expand your shop? Looking for staff, a supplier, a manufacturer, a distributor or customers? Have a skill,
a shop space, a product or money to invest? Post it in one minute. No forms to fill, just tap your answers.

- Post an opportunity: need something, offer something, partner, invest or announce.
- Discover opportunities from businesses near you: latest, nearby, trending, recommended.
- Talk directly in chat. No middlemen, no fees.
- Build trust with a business profile, response rate and completed deals.
- Works in Hindi and English.

For shop owners, home businesses, manufacturers, traders, farmers, students, freelancers, influencers, investors, mentors and
professionals. If you want to earn through business, FMBP is for you.
